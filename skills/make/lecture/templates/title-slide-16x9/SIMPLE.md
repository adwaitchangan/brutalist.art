# Simple Brutalist lecture opener

This is the minimal alternative, not the default: Bear subsequently approved the populated series layout with entry pointer and credits. It has exactly three replaceable fields: portrait, film title and brief description of what the film teaches. No series header, episode numbering, presenter/narrator credits, footer, QR code or event information. See README.md for the lecture default.

16:9, 1920 × 1080. Show the complete slide immediately and hold for 2–3 seconds before the lecture's framing beat. Description: one short phrase or sentence, no paragraph; second line is optional. Replace the portrait with an existing image from `brutalist.art/professor-bear/` unless another presenter is specified. Placeholder labels are editor notes, never export copy.

Live SVG text and named groups remain editable. Font families: EB Garamond (title), Inter (description). This is an SVG template, not an InDesign package. Other channel/brand variants are out of scope. Existing videos and the canonical lecture skill have not been rewritten or rebuilt.

The lecture skill now includes a static identity first beat before the hesitant writer. The rest of the film still teaches visually. If this minimal alternative is explicitly chosen, film-specific series orientation belongs in the following framing beat and must remain clear within 10–15 seconds.

Preview exported with Inkscape and visually inspected for spacing, clipping and safe margins.
