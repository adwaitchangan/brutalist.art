## B04 · MECHANISM · ACT 1 · 18.21s
The first design decision is the shape of every reel. Cold open on the
Claude composer. Beat two is a hesitant writer, correcting a real
misconception into the reel's actual claim. The body illustrates. The
verdict is an artifact card. The handoff types a paste-ready prompt. The
outro restates the title. Same six moves, every episode.

## B05 · MECHANISM · ACT 2 · 20.67s
Second decision. The Claude interface only appears where the interface is
the subject — cold open, ask beats, verdict, handoff, outro. Every other
beat has to ILLUSTRATE its concept: a diagram, a Manim fragment, an Onda
code block, a Remotion illustration. Two composer beats in a row is the
smell. Seven videos of composer wallpaper is the failure this law exists
to prevent.

## B06 · MECHANISM · ACT 3 · 16.88s
Third decision. Whenever the reel is about to show a generated visual, it
first shows the Claude prompt that made it. Composer beat with the ACTUAL
prompt typed in, then the graphic. Never a rendered chart alone — always
the ask, then the receipt. That habit is why this brand feels like the
tool it's teaching, not a slideshow about it.
