## B03 · FRAMEWORK · The mode router — 20.21 s

One router, four modes. Dash-repo dispatches to git-explainer's analysis engine. Dash-paper dispatches to ai-paper. Dash-content asks what has changed since the material was published. Dash-capability runs the thing and shows the real output — often side by side against a competitor. That last mode is the whole reason this skill exists. No vendor channel will ever ship it.

## B07 · FALSIFIABILITY · Where it bites — 27.75 s

Here is where the skill bites. It leans on two sibling engines — git dash-explainer and ai dash-paper — that aren't shipped inside this toolkit snapshot. Point dash-repo or dash-paper at a target and the router has nowhere to dispatch. The fix is to install those skills, not to fake the analysis. Second bite: the generation honesty law. Never generate an image that poses as evidence of a fact. That's a hard ceiling — it rules out most of what makes a video pop, and the skill accepts the trade because the credibility is the whole product.
