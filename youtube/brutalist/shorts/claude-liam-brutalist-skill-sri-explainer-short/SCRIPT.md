### B06 · MECHANISM · Register (`SkillTeardownMechanism916`) — 22.63s

> Second decision. The Sridhar register — derived directly from reading quantum dash mechanics
> dash vol one, not invented. The move you hear most in the book is negation before affirmation.
> State what a result is NOT before affirming what it IS. Short punch sentences after a longer
> explanatory one. Concrete physical scale over abstract claim. A number always anchored to
> something felt — an e V, a room temperature k B T — never a bare symbol.

### B07 · MECHANISM · Derivation-compression law (`SkillTeardownMechanism916`) — 21.58s

> Third decision, and it is a compression rule. Never narrate every algebra line of a derivation
> — that is lecture capture, not an explainer. Extract the two to four points where something
> is actually decided — the moment a continuous quantity collapses to a discrete one, the moment
> a boundary condition kills a term — and give each its own illustrated beat. One beat per
> genuine turn, never one beat per algebra step.
