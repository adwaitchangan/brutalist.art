## B04 · MECHANISM · 27.38 s · ClaudeCodeBeat916

> The ranker has one job: put the right lead at the top. Three trust tiers.
> An id hit is worth three points. A synonym hit is worth two. A hit anywhere
> else in the haystack — the description, the folder, the props — is worth
> one. Then it re-weights by inverse document frequency. That means a rare
> word like sankey outranks a common word like flow, so 'sankey flow' finds
> FinanceSankey and not every SourceFlow in the set. And a derived-only
> entry loses half a point, so a scene that actually describes itself
> outranks a guessed one.
