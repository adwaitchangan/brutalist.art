## What this reel does NOT do

- **It does not run the guests skill for real.** Running the skill requires
  (a) a guest's video file, (b) a `screen-clean` pass on that recording, and
  (c) the guest's actual signature on `REVIEW.md` via GATE G. Zero of those
  exist in this free unattended pipeline. Per SELF-DEMO LAW's feasibility
  fallback (ai-explainer §skill-teardown modifier), this teardown is
  source-backed: every claim traces to the SKILL.md, verbatim quotes appear
  on-screen with citation, and the Your Turn prompt asks the viewer to
  *plan* a dry-run — not execute one.
- **No guest is named, recorded, transcribed, transcoded, published, or
  uploaded.** The example beat sheets under the workspace's `examples/` were
  read only for structural leads (credited in SOURCES.md); none of their
  content, results, or human signatures appear here.
- **No paid APIs.** Kokoro `am_onyx` for every narration beat, Remotion for
  every visual beat, all local and free.
