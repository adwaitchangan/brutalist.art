## B09 · ClaudeVerdictArtifact — six lines.
- 0.05 artifact card scales in, filling frame.
- 0.15 heading `A gate. A crop. A flag. A sheet.` lands.
- 0.25 six verdict lines reveal one at a time.

Lines:
1. Shared preprocessor called by `fellows`, `guests`, and anything else that
   drops a recorded human into a reel — format is orthogonal to relationship.
2. Pass 1 is a gate: decode audio with `ffmpeg -af volumedetect`; below
   -40 dB, STOP.
3. Pass 2 crops the measured taskbar (~72 px), pads with cream bars
   (`#FAF9F5`), and never touches the top.
4. Pass 3 samples real frames at output size; illegible screen shares are a
   finding, not a defect to paper over.
5. Pass 4 flags every incidental item on screen and waits for a human — never
   auto-blurs.
6. Deliverable to the caller: cleaned 1920×1080 video + contact sheet + a
   measured-numbers report.
