## B06 — The Sridhar register — negate before affirm (SkillTeardownMechanism)

> Second decision. The Sridhar register — derived directly from reading
> quantum dash mechanics dash vol one, not invented. The move you hear
> most in the book is negation before affirmation. State what a result
> is NOT before affirming what it IS. Short punch sentences after a
> longer explanatory one. Concrete physical scale over abstract claim.
> A number always anchored to something felt — an eV, a room temperature
> k B T — never a bare symbol.

## B07 — DERIVATION-COMPRESSION LAW (SkillTeardownMechanism)

> Third decision, and it is a compression rule. Never narrate every
> algebra line of a derivation — that is lecture capture, not an
> explainer. Extract the two to four points where something is actually
> decided — the moment a continuous quantity collapses to a discrete
> one, the moment a boundary condition kills a term — and give each its
> own illustrated beat. One beat per genuine turn, never one beat per
> algebra step.

## B08 — GOLDEN-TEST-ON-SCREEN LAW (SkillTeardownMechanism)

> Fourth decision, and this is the honesty gate. Any number shown on
> screen carries its own check. The ratio it satisfies, the limit it
> reduces to, the conservation law it obeys — spoken and shown. This is
> sharper than the parent's generic double check law because the book's
> own system prompt makes it non negotiable. Never assert a numeric
> result is physically correct without an independent checkable
> comparison on screen.

## B09 — HANDOFF-FROM-THE-CHAPTER LAW (SkillTeardownMechanism)
