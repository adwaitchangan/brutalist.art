## What this reel does NOT do

- **It does not run the fellows skill for real.** Running the skill requires
  (a) a fellow's video file, (b) the fellow's explicit persistent-voice
  approval, and (c) Professor Bear's actual signature on a `NOTES.md`. Zero
  of those exist in this free, unattended pipeline. Per SELF-DEMO LAW's
  feasibility fallback (ai-explainer §skill-teardown modifier), this teardown
  is source-backed: every claim traces to the SKILL.md, verbatim quotes appear
  on-screen with citation, and the Your Turn prompt asks the viewer to *plan*
  a dry-run — not execute one.
- **No fellow's video is transcoded, transcribed, published, or uploaded.**
  The example beat sheets under `examples/` were read only for structural
  leads (credited in SOURCES.md); none of their content, results, or human
  signatures appear here.
- **No paid APIs.** Kokoro `am_onyx` for every narration beat, Remotion for
  every visual beat, all local and free.
