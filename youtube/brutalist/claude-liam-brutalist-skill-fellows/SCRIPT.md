## What this reel does NOT do

- **No real fellows demo is run.** Running the skill for real requires: (a) a fellow's
  video file, (b) the fellow's explicit voice approval, (c) Professor Bear's actual
  signature on a `NOTES.md`. Zero of those exist in this free, unattended pipeline.
  Per SELF-DEMO LAW's feasibility fallback, this teardown is source-backed: every
  claim traces to the SKILL.md, verbatim quotes appear on-screen with citation,
  and the Your Turn prompt asks the viewer to PLAN a dry-run (not execute one).
- **No fellow's video is transcoded, transcribed, published, or uploaded.** The
  example beat sheets under `examples/` were read only for structural leads and
  are credited in SOURCES.md; none of their content, results, or human signatures
  appear here.
- **No paid APIs are called.** Kokoro `am_onyx` for every narration beat, Remotion
  for every visual beat, all local and free.

## Register notes

The register modifier for fellows is Teardown-**warm**. Because this reel talks
*about* fellows (rather than being one), critique is calibrated at the design
level — not at any fellow. The verdict beat lands on the skill's own side:
the whole apparatus (GATE N, the FELLOW'S-WORK carve-out, the persistent voice,
the HAI outro) exists to protect the fellow's invisible work from being warped
by the frame around it. Skip a mechanism and you break that protection.
