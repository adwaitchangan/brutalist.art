## beat-B01

The spinner itself, a terracotta quill writes one short stroke, lifts and starts again, every 1.6 seconds with a status word beside it. Use it when the point is that the model is writing.

## beat-B02

Pages three scattered sheets and the quill writes each one line by line, the nib writing the stroke. Scribbles not letters, so the type gate has nothing to read, and the voice says what is being written.

## beat-B03

## beat-B04
