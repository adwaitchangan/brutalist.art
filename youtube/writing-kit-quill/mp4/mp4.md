## writing-kit-quill-slate

Hege, this is Liam in for Bear, a new set in the brutalist toolkit, The Writing Quill. It began as the little spinner in the clawed app, The Terracotta Quill that turns while clawed writes. Now it is six icons, four emotion animations, and a man-imwriting kit. Here's each one. Six terms, a Quill, the drawn feather pen nib first, and in Quill, the dark bottle with one Terracotta drop, a scribble, a wavy stroke that reads as handwriting never as type. An outline, a branching tree from one trunk, a Terracotta dotted every tip. A spinner, the apps writing mark a Terracotta Quill looping one stroke, and the writing kit, all of it in man-im and in remotion. The icons first, six originals drawn in the house line, 24 by 24, stroke two, a Quill and in Quill, a Quill on a scroll, a Quill with pages, and two more, a plain scroll and a scribbled page. They sit in the canonical set, so art icons Quill finds them, and they drop straight onto an icon row. The spinner itself, a Terracotta Quill writes one short stroke, lifts, and starts again, every 1.6 seconds with a status word beside it. Use it when the point is that the model is writing. Pages, three scattered sheets, and the Quill writes each one line by line, the nib writing the stroke. Scribbles, not letters, so the type gate has nothing to read, and the voice says what is being written. The scroll, it unrolls, then the Quill draws an outline, one trunk, two forks, a Terracotta dotted every tip, the reference drawing as emotion. One page, the draft grows down the sheet, eight lines, the Quill always at the last stroke, good for a long beat where something is being written while the voice explains. And in Manum, the same objects as drawn primitives in the Showtale kit, paper, Quill, scribbles on and write lines, which hops the nib to each stroke and rides it as it draws. It paces to the narration like every other kit seen. The outline tree and the Inquil in the isometric kit grow tree draws each branch with the Quill and lands the Terracotta tips last. This icons four cards, two Manum rigs, one Quill. Your turn, paced this into Claude, add one writing beat to my explainer, the moment where who drafts what. Pick the best of the brutalist writing kit for it, the Quill writer pages, scroll, page, or spinner card, or the Manum Quill with right lines, and write the beat's narration and the exact props or scene code. Then check two things yourself, is the stroke a scribble, never a word, and is Terracotta only the drop, the tips, or the spinner. Write in Quill at Nick Bear Brown.
